Self-verify the build against the ticket before handoff.

Checklist:

1. Every acceptance criterion is either satisfied or honestly marked unmet.
2. Evidence required by the ticket exists or is explicitly missing.
3. Diff stays inside scope.
4. Tests related to the change pass, or failures are explained.
5. No obvious clean-code regressions were introduced.

Update the build report accordingly. Keep the final status line accurate:

```text
BUILD STATUS: COMPLETE
```

or

```text
BUILD STATUS: BLOCKED
```
